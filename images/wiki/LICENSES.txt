Wiki icons
==========

The current ten Wiki icons are original SVG artwork created for this site.
No third-party icon geometry is used in these files.
Earlier icon sources and their license notices are preserved in Git history.

Shared style
------------
Canvas: 64 x 64 viewBox, displayed at 48 x 48 px.
Main outlines: 2 units, rounded caps and joins.
Detail lines: 1.5 units where needed.
Charcoal: #29252a
Red: #d9363e
Warm white: #fff7f3
Muted red: #b75b62
Soft coral: #ed8178
Flat fills only; no gradients, shadows, textures, external fonts or images.
Keep each subject distinct at small sizes.
