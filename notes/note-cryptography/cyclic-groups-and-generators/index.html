循环群与生成元

2023.06.02

Cyclic Groups and Generators.

Before stepping into the definition of cyclic groups, we introduce the definition of order of a group element.

Definition. The order of a group element g∈Gg\in \mathbb{G}g∈G is the smallest positive integer rrr such that gr=1g^r=1gr=1, and is denoted by ord(g)ord(g)ord(g).

Note the distinction between the two concepts the order of a group and the order of a group element.

Definition. If an element g∈Gg\in \mathbb{G}g∈G has order ∣G∣|\mathbb{G}|∣G∣, then G\mathbb{G}G is a cyclic group and can be denoted by G=⟨g⟩\mathbb{G}=\langle g \rangleG=⟨g⟩. We call ggg the generator of G\mathbb{G}G.

Therefore, if G\mathbb{G}G is a cyclic group and ∣G∣=m|\mathbb{G}|=m∣G∣=m, there must exists a generator g∈Gg\in\mathbb{G}g∈G that could generate the whole group by constantly multiplying itself, i.e., {g,g2,...,gm}=G\{g, g^2, ..., g^m\}=\mathbb{G}{g,g2,...,gm}=G.

Eureka moment!

之前搞 OI 的时候在数论里学习到了原根 (primitive root) 的概念，然而并不是很明白。到大学学到生成元 (generator) 的概念突然有了醍醐灌顶之感。实际上，原根和生成元的概念是一体两面。

若一个正整数 mmm 有原根 ggg，那么就意味着群 (Z/m)∗(\mathbb{Z}/m)^{*}(Z/m)∗ 是一个循环群，且它的生成元为 ggg。

正整数 mmm 有原根 (群 (Z/m)∗(\mathbb{Z}/m)^{*}(Z/m)∗ 为循环群) 的充要条件为：它能表达为下列形式之一 2,4,pn,2pn2,4,p^n,2p^n2,4,pn,2pn，其中 ppp 为奇素数。且若 mmm 有原根，其原根的个数为 φ(φ(m))\varphi(\varphi(m))φ(φ(m))。部分证明 (mmm 为质数) 见我的 CnBlogs: 循环群；这里还提到，任意质数阶群 (group of prime order) 一定是循环群。
