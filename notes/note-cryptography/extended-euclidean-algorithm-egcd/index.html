2023.06.02

Extended Euclidean Algorithm (eGCD).

Euclidean Algorithm (辗转相除法) is one of the oldest and most widely known algorithms. It is a O(log⁡n)O(\log n)O(logn) method of computing the greatest common divisor (GCD) of two integers a,ba,ba,b.

According to Bezout's Identity, there exists integers XXX and YYY that Xa+Yb=gcd⁡(a,b)Xa+Yb=\gcd(a,b)Xa+Yb=gcd(a,b) given a,ba,ba,b. The extended Euclidean algorithm is the method of computing such XXX and YYY.

Assuming two adjacent levels in the GCD recursive process:

known X′X'X′ known Y′Y'Y′ bbb a%ba\% ba%b
Xa+Yb=X′b+Y′(a%b)Xa+Yb=X′b+Y′(a−⌊ab⌋b)Xa+Yb=Y′a+(X′−Y′⌊ab⌋)b \begin{aligned} Xa+Yb&=X'b+Y'(a\% b) \\ Xa+Yb&=X'b+Y'(a-\lfloor \frac{a}{b}\rfloor b) \\ Xa+Yb&=Y'a+(X'-Y'\lfloor \frac{a}{b} \rfloor)b \\ \end{aligned} Xa+YbXa+YbXa+Yb​=X′b+Y′(a%b)=X′b+Y′(a−⌊ba​⌋b)=Y′a+(X′−Y′⌊ba​⌋)b​

Using eGCD to get Inverse

Given b,Nb,Nb,N, if gcd⁡(b,N)=1\gcd(b,N)=1gcd(b,N)=1, bbb is invertible modulo NNN. We could apply eGCD algorithm to find multiplicative inverse of bbb modulo NNN.

Run the above function exgcd(b,N,x,y), we could obtain xxx and yyy that xb+yN=gcd⁡(b,N)=1xb+yN=\gcd(b,N)=1xb+yN=gcd(b,N)=1. Therefore xb≡1(modN)xb\equiv 1 \pmod{N}xb≡1(modN). xxx is a multiplicative inverse of bbb modulo NNN.
