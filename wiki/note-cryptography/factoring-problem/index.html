整数分解问题

2023.06.05

Factoring Problem.

Factoring could be viewed as the inverse operation of multiplication. Multiplying two primes ppp and qqq as N=p×qN=p\times qN=p×q is easy, while factorizing NNN to derive ppp and qqq is hard.

Factoring the product of two primes: Given N=pqN=pqN=pq, where p,qp,qp,q are primes, find ppp and qqq.

Factoring Experiment

Cryptographically, the hardness of the factoring problem is indicated by the factoring experiment FactorA,Genmodulus(n)\mathtt{Factor}_{\mathscr{A}, Genmodulus}(n)FactorA,Genmodulus​(n), where GenModulus is an efficient (polynomial-time) algorithm that, on input 1n1^n1n, outputs (N,p,q)(N,p,q)(N,p,q) where N=pqN=pqN=pq, and p,qp,qp,q are nnn-bit primes except with negligible probability.

Adversary A\mathscr{A}A with NNN needs to efficiently compute the corresponding p,qp,qp,q.

Definition. Factoring is hard relative to GenModulus if for all probabilistic polynomial-time algorithm A\mathscr{A}A, there exists a negligible ε(n)\varepsilon(n)ε(n) that Pr⁡[FactorA,GenModulus(n)=1]≤ε(n)\Pr[\mathtt{Factor}_{\mathscr{A}, GenModulus}(n)=1]\leq \varepsilon(n)Pr[FactorA,GenModulus​(n)=1]≤ε(n).

The Factoring Assumption is the assumption that there exists a GenModulus relative to which factoring is hard.

Eureka moment!

这里对数学问题困难性的定义又与上面的算法数论 (algorithmic number theory) 或计算机科学领域的定义不同，是以实验来描述的。是不是很有加密学的特色？

这里还要注意一个概念：密码学假设 (assumption)，它将某个数学问题的困难性归约到实验中的一个高效 (efficient) 算法的存在性上。对于大数质因数分解问题 (Factoring problem)，其困难性被某个高效的 GenModulus 算法的存在保证。
