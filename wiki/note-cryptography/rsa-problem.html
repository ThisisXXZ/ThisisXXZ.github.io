2023.06.05

RSA Problem.

Given a modulus NNN and integer e>2e>2e>2, such that gcd⁡(e,φ(N))=1\gcd(e, \varphi(N))=1gcd(e,φ(N))=1.

By the Corollary 2 of the Fermat's Little Theorem (see it in P2), we know that exponentiation to the eee-th power mod NNN is a permutation on (Z/N)∗(\mathbb{Z}/N)^{*}(Z/N)∗;

For any y∈(Z/N)∗y\in (\mathbb{Z}/N)^{*}y∈(Z/N)∗, let [y1/emod  N][y^{1/e} \mod{N}][y1/emodN] be the unique element which yields yyy when raised to the eee-th power mod NNN, i.e., the eee-th root of yyy mod NNN.

If e⋅d≡1(modφ(N))e\cdot d\equiv 1\pmod{\varphi(N)}e⋅d≡1(modφ(N)), then raising to the ddd-th power is the inverse of raising to the eee-th power, that is, for any g∈(Z/N)∗g\in (\mathbb{Z}/N)^{*}g∈(Z/N)∗, (ge)d≡g(modN)(g^e)^d \equiv g\pmod{N}(ge)d≡g(modN). Therefore, if y=gey=g^ey=ge, y1/e=g=yd(modN)y^{1/e}=g=y^d\pmod{N}y1/e=g=yd(modN).

RSA problem is essentially finding eee-th root of yyy mod NNN: Given N,e,yN,e,yN,e,y, compute [y1/emod  N][y^{1/e}\mod{N}][y1/emodN]. Note that e>2e>2e>2 and gcd⁡(e,φ(N))=1\gcd(e, \varphi(N))=1gcd(e,φ(N))=1, gcd⁡(y,N)=1\gcd(y, N)=1gcd(y,N)=1.

The RSA Assumption is the assumption that there exists a GenRSA algorithm relative to which the RSA problem is hard. Note that GenRSA can be constructed from GenModulus.

RSA and Factoring

The hardness of RSA problem could be reduced to the hardness of factoring problem, which is to say, one could efficiently solve RSA problem if he could efficiently solve factoring problem.

Assume there exists an efficient algorithm A\mathscr{A}A that could factorize NNN into two distinct primes p,qp,qp,q, then we could construct an efficient A′\mathscr{A}'A′ that could solve RSA problem.

  • A′\mathscr{A}'A′ runs A(N)\mathscr{A}(N)A(N) and obtain two distinct odd primes p,qp,qp,q that N=pqN=pqN=pq.
  • A′\mathscr{A}'A′ simply computes φ(N)=(p−1)(q−1)\varphi(N)=(p-1)(q-1)φ(N)=(p−1)(q−1).
  • A′\mathscr{A}'A′ computes d≡e−1(modφ(N))d\equiv e^{-1}\pmod{\varphi(N)}d≡e−1(modφ(N)) with extended Euclidean algorithm.
  • A′\mathscr{A}'A′ could compute y1/ey^{1/e}y1/e mod NNN efficiently by computing ydy^dyd mod NNN.
Eureka moment!

RSA 问题本质上是求模意义下的 eee 次方根。注意 RSA 问题中的模数 NNN 是两个不同的大奇质数 p,qp,qp,q 之积。

RSA 问题的巧妙之处在于，利用群的 closure 性质与费马小定理可得，模意义下的 eee 次方根与模意义下的 ddd 次方幂是等价的；其中 gcd⁡(e,φ(N))=1\gcd(e, \varphi(N))=1gcd(e,φ(N))=1, d≡e−1(modφ(N))d\equiv e^{-1} \pmod{\varphi(N)}d≡e−1(modφ(N)). 也就是说，如果敌手得知 φ(N)\varphi(N)φ(N)，那么他就能通过计算得出 ddd，从而轻松解决 RSA 问题。

然而得知 φ(N)\varphi(N)φ(N) 也是困难的；这由 Factoring problem 的困难性保证。对于只有两个质因数 p,qp,qp,q 的模数 NNN, φ(N)=φ(pq)=φ(p)φ(q)=(p−1)(q−1)\varphi(N)=\varphi(pq)=\varphi(p)\varphi(q)=(p-1)(q-1)φ(N)=φ(pq)=φ(p)φ(q)=(p−1)(q−1)。所以，得到 φ(N)\varphi(N)φ(N) 的前提是成功将 NNN 分解为 p,qp,qp,q。

著名的 RSA 公钥加密就应用了这一性质：在该加密中，公钥 pk=epk=epk=e, 私钥 sk=dsk=dsk=d。加密 Encpk(m)=meEnc_{pk}(m)=m^eEncpk​(m)=me，解密 Decsk(c)=cdDec_{sk}(c)=c^dDecsk​(c)=cd (均在 mod NNN 意义下)。RSA 问题的困难性保证了任意不持有私钥的敌手无法由密文 c=mec=m^ec=me 破解消息 mmm (即，得到 ccc 在模 NNN 意义下的 eee 次方根)。
